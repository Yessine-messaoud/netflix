# Task 2 - EDA findings

- Movies make up 70% of the catalogue (6123 movies vs 2663 TV shows).
- Top 3 countries: United States, India, United Kingdom. The US alone contributes 3240 titles.
- Most common categories: International Movies, Dramas, Comedies.
- Additions peaked in 2019 (2013 titles); 2021 data stops in September.
- Most frequent rating is TV-MA; 46% of titles target adults.
- Median movie runs 98 min; 67% of TV shows have only one season.
- Busiest month for additions: July.
- Median gap from release to Netflix: 1 year(s); 37% were added the year they came out.
